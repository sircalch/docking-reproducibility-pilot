# Manuscript outline: reproducibility properties of a small multiblanco docking pilot

## Contribution

Demonstrate how a transparent software workflow separates data integrity, rank
agreement and retrospective discrimination summaries in a small multiblanco
pilot without converting computational prioritization into biological claims.

## Results to report

Use only the aggregate results in `results/PILOT-RESULTS.md`, cite the exact
software release/DOI for each companion package, and describe the pilot size and
source exclusions.

## Required limitations

- Small 24-ligand, three-seed pilot per target.
- Retrospective DUD-E labels; no prospective validation.
- No redistributed raw docking artifacts.
- No experimental confirmation.
- No cross-target pooling.

## What is still needed before journal submission

Independent rerun from legally accessible inputs, a complete methods audit, and
coauthor review of every reported value and interpretation.
